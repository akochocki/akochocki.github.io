About

My research focuses on branching processes and models of biological populations. I am interested in branching with interactions and genealogical dependence, leading to feedback between growth and tree structure. My recent work explores branching coupled to genealogy-dependent resource flows, with some similarity to catalytic processes.

I recently completed my PhD in physics at Michigan State University. My research involved the simulation of particle branching processes, statistical inference for point processes, and numerical modeling of complex systems. I am currently teaching Introduction to Electricity and Magnetism, and Electrodynamics at Loyola Marymount University. I will be seeking postdoctoral positions for Fall 2027.
